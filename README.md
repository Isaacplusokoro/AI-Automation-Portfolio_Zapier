### 2. AI Inquiry Classifier and Router: Zapier
A no-code automation that reads Google Form submissions, classifies them with AI (Sales, Support, KYC, Spam, Other), logs each result to Google Sheets, and routes sales inquiries to the right inbox.

**Highlights:** confidence threshold, human-review guardrails, and a debugging log of problems and fixes.
